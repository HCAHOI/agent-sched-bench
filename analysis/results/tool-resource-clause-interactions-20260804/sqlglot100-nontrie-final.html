Evidence vs inference

Observed

  • Poset latency: +1 correct command out of 1,792; CI crosses zero.
  • Full subset ties Current; every bounded-order ablation is at or below Current.
  • Frozen poset transfers as CPU −0.09 pp, RSS +0.33 pp, Disk −0.39 pp.

Inference

  • Shared argument interactions are common enough to affect predictions.
  • They are not stable enough to replace the current unified KB on this cohort.
Takeaway

Stop feature/kernel tuning on SQLGlot100. The experiment falsifies “more flexible interaction matching is sufficient”: it changes many decisions, but helpful and harmful changes nearly cancel and resource transfer is mixed.

Any next claim needs a fresh repository/task stream and a new predeclared decision—not another weight, alpha, threshold, or parser tweak selected on these exposed labels.
