## The λ reframe (important, changes what the screen means)

The concurrency-pressure λ the spec assumed is NOT available: apparent
occupancy in fresh-277 is the collection harness's `--concurrency 2`
flag (peak 2 simultaneous tasks; the apparent tail is sub-ms read_file
collisions). Pricing off it would have produced a false negative. The
multi-tenant CONTENTION axis is therefore a STRUCTURAL NEGATIVE on
this corpus. But a genuinely causal price exists: per-call KV
footprint from `llm_call.data.prompt_tokens` (all 13410 calls; 1.9k→
85k tokens, 44x range; median 13.4x growth WITHIN a task). Since KV
bytes are linear in resident tokens, this IS the swap price per call,
and it is known at call start (context frozen during a call) — so the
arm uses no hindsight and is implementable, not an oracle.
**Reframed question: the certified policy prices every call at ONE
fixed kv_cost while real footprints vary 44x.** A positive screen does
NOT license deployment — it triggers a separate certified decision
replay (paired vs frozen policy, rho=0.94, permutation per kv cell).
