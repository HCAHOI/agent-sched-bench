This is the authoritative checkpoint for the 2026-07-20 reframe. Read

## 1. The criterion changed: policy utility, not estimator accuracy

The paper is organized around decision utility at the frozen operating point
`rho=0.94`, reported as seconds saved. MAE, log score, PIT, CRPS, and coverage
diagnose an estimator; they do not rank deployable policies.

WTN is the cautionary exhibit that makes C2 concrete: screen-learned wrapper
normalization improved MAE by about 21 ms but was directionally harmful by
59.6 s/277 at kv3500. The certification gate correctly refused to ship it.
Never present the WTN MAE table as a policy leaderboard.

## 2. One object, one lifecycle, two priced actions

The single object is the per-workload certified conditional-residual survival
curve. It has two composed decision points on one tool-call lifecycle:

- `g`: swap-out trigger — when staying resident stops paying.
- `s`: pre-restore start — when bringing the swapped KV back starts paying.
- `s >= g` by construction: pre-restore exists only for calls already swapped.

They share node samples, cross-fit discipline, expected-cost semantics,
certificate, and hazard/utility code path. They are not two parallel modules.
The composition is what turns the prior from a one-off trigger heuristic into a
primitive with two priced consumers.

### Current non-cd-skip policy stack

| Policy layer | Compared with | Decision-utility result at rho=0.94 |
|---|---|---|
| Deadline | — | Reference |
| H1-certified swap trigger | Deadline | +66.7 s/277 @kv3500; +150.6 s/277 @kv5000 |
| Pre-restore composed after swap | Same trigger without pre-restore | +156.2 s/277 @kv3500, CI [60.4, 256.6]; +317.9 s/277 @kv5000, CI [181.0, 460.4] |

The pre-restore numbers are incremental net gains, not totals versus deadline.
Do not add the two rows without a joint end-to-end artifact. Pre-restore is
certified offline under both trigger sources and near-invariant to trigger
choice, but it is not shipped until GPU live validation prices real transfer
contention.

The underlying production baseline has `skip_leading_cd=false`. Hardcoded
cd-skip is only an oracle/positive-control row, never a method.

Banked:

Not yet earned:

- Head-to-heads against deadline, Continuum-style TTL, and ThunderAgent over at
  least three load levels and at least two workloads.

Continuum and ThunderAgent have not been executed. Continuum has no released
code and needs a fidelity-checked TTL reimplementation; ThunderAgent has public
code but still needs the planned 8B run.

### C2 — certification decides what conditioning ships

Decision utility, not estimator accuracy, is the gate:

- WTN: accuracy improved, utility regressed; rejected.
- Atom decomposition and stable-atom screening: rejected.
- Calibration supplies the complement: the shipped prior is honest where the
  policy consumes it.
- W3-4 online gate supplies lifecycle machinery: replay an anytime-valid gate
  against the once-only offline permutation gate on identical paired deltas.

### C3 — the policy space is closed

Banked:

- Any elapsed-only k-check policy collapses to one stopping time; k=2 and k=1
  have a 0.0 ms value gap over 670 nodes x 10 kv cells before check overhead.
- Boundary identity changes decisions but adds no measurable information.
- Footprint-aware pricing is closed for the self-footprint axis: kv3500
  headroom 8.75 s/277, simultaneous CI [-85.00, 101.90], whose upper bound is
  below the frozen 156 s/277 pre-restore bar. Verdict: DROP.
- Multi-tenant contention is not closed: fresh-277 has no shared-KV contention
  signal, so only the W5-7 harness may support that claim.

Footprint pricing was a separate reframe from WTN. The original occupancy-price
idea could not be measured honestly on this corpus, so it became per-call
resident-footprint pricing. It changed the action price `lambda_i`, not the
survival estimator. Its DROP does not weaken the composed swap/pre-restore
stack.
