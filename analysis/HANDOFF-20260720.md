## Killed, with reasons

Atom decomposition, stable-atom screening, key normalization (WTN),
boundary evidence, k>1 re-checks, the contention axis on this corpus,
and Terminal-Bench expansion — each with the measurement that killed
it. → `CLOSED-QUESTIONS.md`

The one to remember: **WTN improved MAE by 21 ms and would have
shipped a −59.6 s regression.** Estimator accuracy does not imply
decision utility; only the replay gate distinguishes them.
